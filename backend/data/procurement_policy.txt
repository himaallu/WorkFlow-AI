# [SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA] Enterprise Procurement Policy v1.2

1. Approval Thresholds
- Purchases below AED 5,000: Manager approval required.
- Purchases between AED 5,000 and AED 25,000: Department Head approval required.
- Purchases between AED 25,000 and AED 100,000: Department Head + Finance approval required.
- Purchases above AED 100,000: Department Head + Finance + Procurement approval required.

2. Vendor Quotations
- All purchases exceeding AED 10,000 require at least three (3) vendor quotations.

3. Preferred Vendors
- TechGlobal and AppleStore are preferred vendors for laptops.
- ErgoChairCo is the preferred vendor for office furniture.
